## ✨ What is Ibra Pill?

Ibra Pill is a small glass capsule in the style of the iPhone Dynamic Island. It sits on your screen, reacts to what you do, and opens when you hover over it. Move it anywhere you like, or put it at the top centre for the classic Dynamic Island look.

Meet **Pilly**, the little mascot who lives inside it. Just say **"Hey Pilly"**. 💙

## 🧩 Features

| | |
|---|---|
| 🎵 **Music** | See what is playing, with the cover, and control it from the pill |
| 🕌 **Prayer times & adhkar** | Prayer times for your city, a countdown to the next prayer, and gentle adhkar reminders |
| 🎙️ **"Hey Pilly" voice commands** | Open apps, set timers, change the volume, search the web and more. Add your own commands too |
| 🤖 **Optional AI** | Add your own free Gemini key and Pilly can answer questions |
| 📋 **Clipboard history** | Find what you copied earlier |
| ⏬ **Downloads** | Watch your downloads in the pill |
| ⏱️ **Timer & reminders** | Focus timer, water and eye-rest reminders |
| 🔒 **Privacy alerts** | See when an app is using your microphone or camera |
| 🌍 **Arabic & English** | Choose your language in Settings |
| 🎨 **Make it yours** | Colours, glass style, size and position |

## ⬇️ Download and install

1. Go to the **[latest release](https://github.com/IbrahimShb/Ibra-Pill/releases/latest)** and download **`IbraPill-Setup.exe`**.
2. Run it. It installs for your user only, no admin rights needed.
3. If Windows shows a blue warning, click **More info** → **Run anyway**.

> **Why the warning?** The app is new and not code-signed yet. Windows and Chrome warn about every new unsigned program. It goes away as more people download it.

Prefer no installer? Download **`IbraPill-Portable.zip`**, unzip it and run `IbraPill.exe`.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 (version 1809 or newer) or Windows 11, 64-bit.

## 🔄 Updates

Open **Settings → Updates** and press **Check now**. A new version installs over the old one and keeps your settings.

## 🔐 Your privacy

- Your settings and clipboard history stay **on your PC**. There are no accounts and no tracking.
- The internet is used only to find your city when you use location features, for downloads you start, to look up a missing song cover (title and artist only, can be turned off), and for the AI if you add your own key.
